# Try-On Diffusion evaluation and collection

Collect only after a responsible person approves the restricted storage and consent process. Run Segmind only after account access, model-specific commercial and output terms, data handling and a spend ceiling are documented. Request written confirmation for commercial hosted inference, display/use of outputs, input and output retention/deletion, processing locations and subprocessors, training use and URL access. Record response date, exact endpoint/model, applicable account/terms version and a restricted evidence reference. The generic model page does not settle these rights. [Segmind model](https://www.segmind.com/models/try-on-diffusion).

Create a StylAI account through the Segmind platform, verify the selected endpoint appears in the account, and contact the support or sales channel listed on the model page. Suggested draft for the account owner to send after review:

> StylAI plans to offer virtual clothing previews in a Brazilian commercial web app. Can you confirm in writing whether our account may use your hosted `try-on-diffusion` API for this purpose and display or let customers save its outputs? Please identify the applicable terms, any output restrictions, whether photos and outputs are used for training, retention/deletion behavior, processing locations, subprocessors, and who can access result URLs. We also need to confirm the billing unit and how failed or retried requests are charged.

Keep the response and applicable terms in restricted records. The request does not require sending photos or signing up for paid inference. The draft has not been sent.

Prepare 24 eligible cases with eight each for tops, bottoms and dresses. Use consenting adults and approved garment variants, with diverse framing, skin tones, body sizes and lighting. Include same-item color/variant checks. Keep a separate negative set for missing consent, expired input, wrong variant, unsupported garment, poor framing and revoked image rights. Validation cases that should fail before dispatch must use local fixtures. Use [the header-only manifest](try-on-diffusion-manifest.example.csv) in restricted storage. Store source photos separately from consent records with random case IDs, restricted access and a deletion date. Never include photos, signed URLs, participant identity or tokens in the repo.

Before running, freeze the dataset and expected variant, pin the adapter and presets, agree quality and budget gates, and review how outputs will be deleted. Review identity and garment fidelity, color/variant, artifacts, latency, billed duration, cost, expiry and private output handling. Proposed pilot acceptance is at least 22/24 acceptable overall with at least 7/8 per category, zero severe identity or privacy failures, and reported p50/p95 latency and cost per accepted preview. Treat this as an exploratory pilot. Log billable failures and unknown submissions, stop at the first spend ceiling or serious privacy/billing incident, and verify reconciliation and deletion.

Publish a sanitized report with aggregate counts, anonymized case IDs, model and adapter versions, metrics, failure taxonomy and reviewed go/revise/no-go. No evaluation has run.
